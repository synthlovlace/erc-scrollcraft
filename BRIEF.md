## The feeling curve

```
1  Precision      clean blueprint lines on dark ground, the plan exists before the space does
2  Weight         raw materials arriving — steel beams, lumber stacks, concrete — the scale of what's coming
3  Grit           construction in motion, framing going up, sparks, dust, the physical reality of building
4  Confidence     the finished space emerging — polished floors, clean fixtures, lights on — transformation complete
5  Resolve        one line, one action — the build starts with a conversation
```

**No two adjacent acts share a feeling.** Precision is cerebral, Weight is physical, Grit is kinetic, Confidence is the payoff, Resolve is the ask. Each earns its slot.
